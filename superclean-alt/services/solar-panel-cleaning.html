What dust actually costs you

A solar panel generates power from the light that reaches the cell. Anything sitting on the glass — dust, bird droppings, pollen, cement dust from nearby construction — blocks part of that light, and output drops accordingly. In a dry, dusty climate like Islamabad's, a layer builds up within weeks of installation and keeps building through the whole dry season.

What makes it worse than it sounds is how panels are wired. Panels in a string operate together, so a heavily soiled or shaded panel can drag down the performance of the ones connected to it, not just its own. That is why an array with a few badly soiled panels — or a couple of bird droppings sitting in the wrong place — can underperform by considerably more than the dirty area would suggest.

The other half of the problem is that cleaning them badly is worse than not cleaning them. Hard tap water dries and leaves mineral spots that block light just like dust did. Abrasive brushes and scourers scratch the anti-reflective coating permanently. High-pressure jets can force water past the seals and into the junction box, and pouring cold water onto hot panels in the middle of the day can crack them outright. We clean arrays across both cities for homes, offices, factories and farms, and the method matters as much as the frequency.

What is included in solar panel cleaning

Priced per panel, with the rate dropping for larger arrays. Everything below is standard.

  • Visual inspection first — panels, frames, mounting and visible cabling checked, and anything cracked or damaged reported to you before we touch it
  • Early morning or evening scheduling — panels are cleaned when cool, never in the heat of the day when thermal shock can crack the glass
  • Dry debris removal — leaves, twigs and loose material cleared before any water goes on
  • Soft-brush washing with purpose-made panel brushes that will not scratch the anti-reflective coating
  • Deionised or filtered water — dries without leaving the mineral spotting that ordinary tap water causes
  • Bird dropping and sap removal — softened and lifted rather than scraped
  • Frame and gap clearing — dirt and debris removed from the panel edges and the gaps where it collects and holds moisture
  • Post-clean check — panels checked for any missed area or residue before we leave

What is not included as standard

These are separate jobs with their own equipment and pricing. We can add any of them to your booking — just ask when you get your quote.

  • Electrical work of any kind — wiring, inverters, isolators, connections or fault diagnosis
  • Repair or replacement of cracked panels, damaged frames or failed seals
  • Removing and refitting panels, or adjusting mounting angles
  • Roof repairs and waterproofing
  • Any array that cannot be reached safely without specialist access equipment
  • Guaranteed removal of permanent staining, delamination or coating damage that is already present

How the job works, step by step

1. Tell us the array

How many panels, roof or ground mounted, roughly what height, and when they were last cleaned. Photos of the installation are very useful.

2. Inspection and access check

We confirm the panels can be reached safely and check for existing damage, which we report to you before starting rather than after.

We check every panel, and tell you what cleaning interval makes sense for your location and how dusty the site actually is.

Frequently asked questions

How much output do dirty panels actually lose?
It varies with how thick the layer is and how the array is wired, but the loss in a dusty climate is far from trivial, and it builds steadily through the dry season. Because panels in a string affect each other, a few badly soiled panels can pull down more output than their own share. The practical test is your own generation figures — most people who track them see a clear step up after a clean.
Can I just wash them with a hose myself?
You can, but it often does more harm than good. Tap water here is hard, and when it dries on the glass it leaves mineral spots that block light exactly like the dust did. Beyond that, high pressure can force water past the seals into the junction box, and spraying cold water on hot panels risks cracking them. If you do it yourself, do it early morning with soft water and a soft brush, never in the middle of the day.
How often should solar panels be cleaned?
In Islamabad and Rawalpindi, every three to four months suits most installations, and more often if you are near a construction site, an unpaved road, or trees that drop sap and attract birds. Through the dry season the build-up is faster. We will tell you what interval your specific site actually needs after seeing it.
Will cleaning void my warranty?
Not when it is done to the manufacturer's method, which is what we follow — soft brushes, no abrasives, no high pressure, deionised water, and never in peak heat. What does risk a warranty is scrubbing with something abrasive, pressure washing, or using harsh chemicals. If you have specific manufacturer instructions, show them to us and we will work to them.
Do you do commercial and factory arrays?
Yes. We clean commercial rooftop arrays and ground-mounted installations for offices, factories, warehouses and farms across both cities, and larger sites are usually best on a scheduled contract. Because we work 24 hours, we can clean early morning before generation ramps up so there is no meaningful loss of production.
What if you find a damaged panel?
We tell you immediately and we do not touch it. We inspect before we start specifically so that any existing cracks, delamination or damaged seals are on record before cleaning rather than discovered afterwards. We do not do electrical or panel repair work — you would need your installer for that — but you will know about it.
