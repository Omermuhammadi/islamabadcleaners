Maintenance costs less than restoration

Most floor damage is avoidable, and it is almost always cheaper to prevent than to repair. The mechanism is simple: grit gets walked onto the floor, and every footstep grinds it against the surface. On marble and stone that produces the microscopic scratching that eventually needs diamond grinding to fix. On vinyl and epoxy it wears through the protective layer until the material underneath starts absorbing dirt. On tile it drives soil into the grout.

A maintenance programme interrupts that cycle. Regular machine scrubbing removes grit before it does damage, buffing keeps the protective layer intact, and periodic sealing or re-coating renews it. A floor on a proper schedule can go many years without ever needing full restoration, and it looks consistently presentable rather than swinging between shabby and freshly restored.

We run floor care programmes for offices, showrooms, clinics, restaurants, schools and larger homes across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, covering marble, granite, tile, vinyl, epoxy, wood and concrete. Frequency is set by traffic — a busy showroom in Blue Area needs a different schedule from a quiet office floor in a Bahria Town commercial block.

What a floor care programme includes

Programmes are built around your floor type, traffic level and budget. A typical schedule includes the following.

  • Floor survey — surface type, current condition, traffic patterns and problem areas identified before anything is scheduled
  • Written maintenance schedule — what gets done, how often, and at what times so it does not disrupt your operation
  • Routine machine scrubbing — rotary or auto-scrubber cleaning on the agreed cycle to remove grit before it causes wear
  • Buffing and burnishing — high-speed polishing that restores gloss and keeps the protective layer even
  • Spray cleaning and spot treatment of traffic lanes, entrances and problem areas between full cycles
  • Periodic sealing or re-coating as the surface requires, so protection is renewed rather than allowed to wear through
  • Grout and edge maintenance — lines, corners and skirting kept up rather than left until they need deep restoration
  • Entrance matting advice — the cheapest single intervention, since most damaging grit walks in through the front door

What is not included as standard

These are separate jobs with their own equipment and pricing. We can add any of them to your booking — just ask when you get your quote.

  • Full restoration of a floor that is already badly damaged — that needs marble polishing or deep tile cleaning first, after which maintenance takes over
  • Replacing tiles, planks, vinyl sheets or damaged sections
  • Sub-floor repair, levelling or moisture problems
  • Wood floor sanding and refinishing
  • Daily general cleaning and bin emptying, which is our janitorial service

How the job works, step by step

1. Site survey

We visit, identify the floor types, look at how the space is actually used, and find where the wear is concentrated.

2. Restore first if needed

If the floor is already worn, it is brought back to a good baseline once. There is no point maintaining a floor that is already damaged.

3. Agree the schedule

We set frequencies and timings — nights, weekends or quiet hours — and put them in writing so you know exactly what happens and when.

4. Run and review

The programme runs on schedule with the same supervisor, and we review the floor periodically and adjust the frequency if traffic changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between floor care and just cleaning the floor?
Daily cleaning removes what is visible on the surface. Floor care protects the floor itself — machine scrubbing to remove abrasive grit, buffing to keep the protective layer even, and periodic sealing to renew it. One keeps the floor looking acceptable this week; the other decides whether you are paying for a full restoration in three years.
Is a maintenance contract actually cheaper?
Over any reasonable period, yes, and usually by a wide margin. Full marble restoration is several times the cost per square foot of routine maintenance, and a maintained floor may never need it. The other saving is that the floor looks presentable all the time rather than only just after an expensive restoration.
How often should our floors be done?
It depends entirely on traffic. High-traffic retail and showroom floors typically need weekly or fortnightly attention; general office areas monthly; low-traffic areas quarterly. We survey the site and recommend a schedule rather than selling you a fixed package, and we adjust it if your usage changes.
Can you work outside business hours?
Yes, and most of our commercial clients prefer it. We work nights, early mornings and weekends so the floors are done and dry before staff or customers arrive. We operate 24 hours a day, so scheduling around your opening times is not a problem.
What floor types do you handle?
Marble, granite and natural stone, ceramic and porcelain tile, vinyl and LVT, epoxy and resin, polished concrete, and sealed wood. Each needs different machines, pads and chemicals — using the wrong combination is how floors get damaged, so we identify the surface properly at the survey before proposing anything.
Our floor is already in bad condition. Can we start with maintenance?
Not straight away, and we would not recommend it. A floor that is already scratched, dull or worn needs bringing back to a good baseline first — marble polishing or deep tile and grout cleaning depending on the surface. Once it is at that baseline, a maintenance programme holds it there. We will tell you honestly at the survey which stage you are at.
