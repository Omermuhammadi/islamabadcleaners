What our house cleaning covers

House cleaning is the service to book when the whole home needs doing properly but you do not need the intensity of a deep clean. It is a systematic, room-by-room clean of the entire house — kitchen degreased and wiped down, bathrooms descaled and sanitised, bedrooms and living rooms dusted from the fans down to the skirting, floors vacuumed and mopped, and interior glass polished. The difference from a deep clean is depth, not coverage: a house clean maintains a home, a deep clean rescues one.

Most of our house cleaning customers in Islamabad and Rawalpindi fall into two groups. The first books a one-off clean before guests arrive, after a busy stretch, or ahead of Eid and family events, when the whole house needs to come up to standard in a single day. The second books us on a schedule — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — as a reliable alternative to keeping domestic staff, with a trained crew, our own equipment and a supervisor who checks the work.

The twin cities are hard on a house. Dust comes in year-round, and in the newer sectors and societies the construction next door never really finishes. A crew with proper machines gets a full house done in hours — not the days it takes one person with a mop — and because we quote from the details and photos you send, the price reflects your actual house, not a one-size guess over the phone.

What is included in a house clean

A standard house clean covers every lived-in room. If you want rooms skipped or extras added, tell us when you book and the quote reflects exactly that.

  • Kitchen — counters, hob, backsplash and cabinet fronts degreased, sink descaled, floor scrubbed
  • Bathrooms — tiles, fittings, glass and WC descaled and sanitised, mirrors polished
  • Bedrooms — dusting from ceiling fans down, furniture wiped, under-bed areas vacuumed where accessible
  • Living and dining areas — all surfaces, electronics dusted, upholstery vacuumed on the surface
  • Floors throughout — vacuumed then mopped or scrubbed to suit the surface
  • Interior glass — windows and partition glass polished on the inside, plus reachable exterior panes
  • Stairs, corridors and entrance — swept, mopped and dusted, including railings and switches
  • Rubbish removal — cleaning waste bagged and taken away with us

What affects the price

  • Size of the house

    A 5-marla home and a 1-kanal home are different jobs. Storeys count too — stairs and extra bathrooms add time.

  • One-off or regular

    Scheduled weekly or fortnightly visits cost less per visit than a one-off, because the house never falls far behind.

  • Kitchens and bathrooms

    The two slowest rooms in any house. Their number moves the price more than total area does.

  • Current condition

    A maintained home cleans faster. If it has been months, the first visit may be quoted as a deep clean, then regular rates apply.

  • Extras on the visit

    Sofa or carpet shampooing, balcony glass, or outdoor areas can be added to the same visit at a combined rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Islamabad?

It depends on the size of the house, the number of kitchens and bathrooms, and its condition, so we quote from the details and photos you send on WhatsApp rather than guessing. You get one fixed number in writing — for a one-off clean, or a per-visit rate if you book a schedule. As a rule, regular visits work out noticeably cheaper per visit than one-off cleans.

What is the difference between house cleaning and deep cleaning?

Coverage is the same — the whole house — but depth is different. A house clean handles the visible, regular layer: dust, floors, bathrooms, kitchen surfaces. A deep clean goes after long-term build-up: grease inside and on top of cabinets, hardened scale, grout lines, and months of grit in corners. If the house has not been properly cleaned in six months or more, book the deep clean first and maintain it with house cleaning after.

Can I book you weekly or monthly instead of keeping a maid?

Yes — that is exactly what our scheduled service is for. You get a trained crew on fixed days, our own equipment and supplies, a supervisor responsible for quality, and no staffing headaches. Fortnightly is our most common schedule for family homes; offices usually go weekly or daily under a janitorial contract.

How long does a full house clean take?

With a properly sized crew, most 5 to 10 marla homes are finished in half a day. Larger houses, or homes with many bathrooms, can take a full day. We tell you the expected duration with the quote, and we would rather send one more cleaner than leave your house half done at sunset.

Do I need to provide anything?

No. Machines, chemicals, cloths and mops all come with the team. If you prefer we use a specific product — for allergies, a delicate surface or personal preference — keep it ready and tell us in advance.

Can you clean while the family is at home?

Yes, and most house cleans happen exactly that way. The team works room by room so your family is never boxed out of the whole house at once. Tell us if anyone is resting, working from home or unwell and we will sequence the rooms around them.

Do you clean before Eid and family events?

Yes — pre-Eid and pre-event weeks are our busiest, so book a few days ahead if you can. If you need the house done urgently, message us anyway: we run extra crews in peak weeks and can often still fit a same-week slot, including evenings.
