Why dining chairs need their own cleaning

Dining chairs collect a very specific kind of dirt. They sit next to food every day, so the fabric picks up oil vapour from hot dishes, splashes of curry and tea, and the constant contact of hands and clothes at the same spots on the seat edge and armrests. Because the staining builds slowly, most families only notice how dark the seats have become when one chair is compared with the spare that never gets used.

Wiping the fabric with a wet cloth pushes this mix of oil and dust deeper into the foam. Proper cleaning works the other way: we vacuum the loose dust out first, pre-treat food and oil marks individually, shampoo the fabric with an upholstery machine, and then pull the moisture and dissolved dirt back out with hot-water extraction. The seat dries firm and clean instead of damp and smelling of detergent.

We clean dining chairs across Islamabad and Rawalpindi at your home — there is no need to send anything away. A set of six to eight chairs is usually done in under two hours, and most bookings combine the chairs with a sofa or carpet clean on the same visit, which is also the cheapest way to do it. Velvet, jute-weave, polyester blends and leatherette are all handled with the method that suits the material.

What is included in dining chair cleaning

We treat each chair individually — fabric, padding and frame. A typical booking covers the following as standard.

  • Vacuuming — seats, backs, arms and the crevices where crumbs collect, before any liquid touches the fabric
  • Stain pre-treatment — food, oil, tea and ink marks treated individually before the main clean
  • Fabric shampoo — upholstery machine agitation that lifts embedded dirt out of the weave
  • Hot-water extraction — dissolved dirt and moisture pulled back out so foam does not stay damp
  • Leatherette and PU seats — cleaned and wiped with the correct products instead of shampoo
  • Wooden and metal frames — wiped down, including legs and stretchers
  • Odour treatment — food smells treated at the source in the fabric
  • Deodorising finish — chairs left fresh, not perfumed

What this service does not include

  • Re-upholstery, foam replacement or spring repair
  • Polishing or refinishing of wooden frames — we clean them, we do not sand or re-lacquer
  • Guaranteed removal of old permanent dye stains or burns — we will tell you honestly what will and will not come out
  • Antique or heritage chairs with fragile joints — we will look at them first and only proceed if it is safe
  • Curtains and other non-seating fabric, which is quoted separately
  • Repair of loose joints or wobbling legs

How it works

  1. Send us a photo and a count

    WhatsApp us how many chairs you have and what the seats are made of — a photo of one chair is enough. We quote per chair, and combining with a sofa or carpet on the same visit costs less.

  2. We inspect and test

    The team checks the fabric type and tests our solution on a hidden spot, especially on velvet and coloured weaves that can bleed.

  3. Clean, chair by chair

    Vacuum, pre-treat, shampoo, extract — each chair is finished before the next one starts, so you can see the difference side by side.

  4. Dry and check

    Seats are damp, not wet, and usable within a few hours. You check every chair before the team leaves.

What affects the price

  • Number of chairs

    Per-chair pricing drops as the count goes up. A full set of eight costs much less per chair than two chairs on their own.

  • Seat material

    Flat polyester weaves are quick; velvet and delicate blends need gentler chemistry and more time. Leatherette is priced separately.

  • Condition and staining

    Chairs cleaned yearly cost less than seats carrying years of oil build-up that need repeat treatment.

  • Fully upholstered vs seat-only

    A chair with an upholstered back and arms is more work than a wooden chair with a padded seat pad.

  • Combining services

    Adding dining chairs to a sofa or carpet booking on the same visit is cheaper than a separate trip.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dining chair cleaning cost in Islamabad?

We price per chair, and the rate depends on the seat material, how stained the fabric is, and how many chairs there are — a set of eight costs less per chair than two alone. Send us a photo of one chair and the count on WhatsApp and we will give you a fixed price for the whole set before any work starts. Combining the chairs with a sofa or carpet clean on the same visit brings the rate down further.

Do you clean the chairs at home or take them away?

At your home. The machines we use for chairs are portable, so the team cleans the full set in your dining room or on a veranda if you prefer. There is no transport risk and nothing leaves the house. A set of six to eight chairs usually takes between one and two hours depending on condition.

How long do the chairs take to dry?

Hot-water extraction pulls most of the moisture back out of the seat, so chairs are damp rather than wet when we finish. In dry weather they are usable in two to four hours; in humid monsoon weeks allow a little longer or let us schedule the clean for the morning so seats dry through the day.

Can you remove curry, oil and tea stains?

Usually yes — food-based stains respond well when they are treated individually before shampooing, which is exactly how we work. What we cannot promise is removal of old stains that have chemically bonded with the dye of the fabric, or marks that were set by ironing or hot washing. We test first and tell you honestly what will come out before you commit.

Do you clean velvet and delicate fabric chairs?

Yes. Velvet, chenille and delicate blends are cleaned with gentler solutions, lower moisture and a hidden-spot colour test first. If a fabric is too fragile for wet cleaning we say so on inspection and offer dry methods instead — we would rather decline than damage a seat.

Do you also clean sofas on the same visit?

Yes, and most customers combine them. The same team and machines handle sofas, armchairs, dining chairs and rugs in one visit, and the combined price is lower than booking each separately. See our sofa cleaning service for what that side of the job covers.

Do you clean office chairs too?

Yes — office and computer chairs are their own service because mesh and swivel bases need different handling, and offices usually book in batches. If you have a home office chair, we can add it to a dining chair booking without a separate call-out.
